implied value = TVL × category mcap/TVL × 7d momentum × tokenless age × chain breadth

The ratio is the median of what the market already pays per dollar of TVL for traded protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median when a category has fewer than three traded comparables. Momentum reads the seven-day TVL change clamped to ±30%, at half weight; age pays 2% a year run with no token, capped at five years; breadth pays 1% per extra chain, capped at six. Wrapped, staked, bridged and exchange products and versioned duplicates are set aside — every watchlist protocol appears regardless. An implied value is this board's claim, not a prediction that a token is issued.
